# Game Overview
Play as Pip, a little animal with many tricks up his sleeve. Befriend, defeat, or interact with the many beasts and flora found in Dreamland to survive. Survive as long as you can throughout Dreamland and discover the mystery of the Anthurium, a mysterious ancient reality-warping plant deity.

The player's goal is to survive until the Anthurium's final bloom; an event that is built up to throughout a round of the game. A round is typically five minutes, but can be slowed down or accelerated through different methods.

The Anthurium itself is a strange, ever-changing collection of plants, which can change the state of the game in various ways. The Anthurium, depending on its mood, can either feed surrounding animals, go on the offensive, spawn more animals, expand and grow, or many other things. Over time, the Anthurium builds up power eventually leading to a BLOOM EVENT, which is a semi-randomly chosen event the player must survive or solve in order to complete the round. (The Anthurium will give visual and auditory cues on which event will occur as the round progresses).

## Pillars
The player is part of the ecosystem
	All entities (including the player) follow the same built in rules; all entities can use items and abilities, suffer from hunger, have prey/predators, etc.
The Anthurium is the centerpiece of the game
	The Anthurium's internal state is constantly in motion, where events that happen to or around the Anthurium change its behavior. The Anthurium will (generally, although the details are a bit more technical) grow more or less aggressive 
The player's state is fluid
	Abilities can be gathered or discarded to change play style.


# Core Gameplay Loop
The player is spawned in an already populated game board, with the Anthurium plant at the center.
